Tester steps · Final pre-alpha round (latest release)

Pre-alpha. Android only. Do these in order. The KickBack, OmniCast, Plaza Brawler and Omni Plaza buttons always download the latest release. Only the apps in this round are listed here; everything else under Apps is for later rounds.

①

Omni Node 0.5.7 (the daemon)

Uninstall any older Omni Node first (0.5.0 and older won't upgrade in place). From 0.5.1 on, Check for updates installs new versions over the top. Install, open it and turn the Network switch on. Allow notifications and "keep running in background". The status card should go green or teal ("Connected to N nearby" / "Looking for nearby people..."). Allow "Nearby devices" if it asks. Then tap Connect apps, unlock, and Copy token. Leave Relay calls off for this round. If the card turns red or the switch won't stay on, report it (step 8). The phone and PC apps share one core, and there's a Check for updates button. Pair a device links your phone to your own PC or another phone with a 6-digit code or QR, and paired devices go quiet to everyone else. Pairing is optional this round. Proven Nov 14, 2026: a desktop node synced with a phone on the same LAN and over the internet (128 reconciles, 0 errors). This build runs daemon 8.33.2, which also carries Plaza Brawler's dual-signed PvP match results.

Download Omni Node v0.5.7-prealpha
②

KickBack Social

Install and accept the tester consent screen. Open Hub settings (gear), choose Remote hub, type 127.0.0.1 and paste the token from step 1. Then make a post and react to one. From Settings you can also import your LPE Halo identity (transfer code) and bring your own public Mastodon posts into KickBack. Use the in-app Bug report button for anything odd.

Download KickBack
③

OmniCast (1:1 calls)

1:1 video calls are now available through your own Omni Node. Call another tester and check how the call screen says it connected (direct, through the router, or relayed through an Omni node). Classroom Mode is still look-only; tell us what you think of the screens. Honest edge: calls between real phones on different networks are still unproven — the Nov 18/19 trial window passed without a confirmed two-phone run, so this is still the big open test. If you manage one, we especially want to hear about it.

Download OmniCast
④

LPE — your Halo

Create the Halo you fight as in Plaza Brawler. In LPE, open Connect apps in Omni Node, copy this phone's node token, paste it into LPE's Node tab, then save your profile from the Creator tab (handles are lowercase). The newest build adds Halo appearance choosers — figure, skin, shirt, pants, shoes, hair colour and style, with an idle animation and the crown cosmetic (merged Nov 16, 2026; tell us how it looks on your phone). LPE ships on the dev-cycle flow: the button opens its build list — sign in, open the newest run, and download the lpe-android artifact.

Get the latest LPE build
⑤

Plaza Brawler

A fighting game by Hactik Games Studios — rebuilt around a new control set. Choose a Zodiac fighter from the twelve Houses. New in this build: the stages are alive — animated backdrops that loop through the match; movement is now an on-screen directional stick instead of separate arrow buttons; and there's a secret unlock — beat Ophiuchus, the 13th-sign boss, in Arcade to unlock the Creepy Man as a fighter. Play offline against the AI, or pair with another player for a PvP match. Controls: HP high punch · LP low punch · HK high kick · LK low kick · crouch · high block and low block · throw when close · special + direction for your sign's elemental abilities. Chain attacks into combos (try LP, LP, HP). Honest edge: PvP was proven phone-vs-laptop on Nov 15 with the previous controls — this build's new-control PvP is ready for your re-test, and results are dual-signed when you share them. To link it to your Omni Node, open Settings and paste the token from step 1; the health check should turn green and your fighter comes from your plaza profile. Try Check for updates too. There's also a Windows build with controller support in the latest release, for playtesting at a PC.

Download Plaza Brawler

Omni Plaza — your Halo's home, alive

New this round: the Plaza itself. Open it and you're in a living plaza scene: companion animals — rabbits, deer, birds and more — live in the scene, idling, walking and wandering between spots while birds take flight and fish swim the pond; the cast rotates through, and the light follows your real time of day, so visit after dark and the plaza is dark too. The Halo you made in LPE (step 4) is the you who lives here. Watch for a bit and tell us: does it feel alive? What did the animals do while you watched?

In the works · Nov 2026

Where things stand, honestly: the desktop Omni Node is field-proven (Nov 14, 2026) and Plaza Brawler PvP is proven phone-vs-laptop (Nov 15, with the previous controls). OmniCast calls between real phones on different networks are still unproven — the Nov 18/19 trial window passed without a confirmed run. Everything below the first two cards is roadmap: designed or being built, not shipped.

Omni Node on your PC — proven

The same Omni Node screen on Windows and Linux (macOS from source): the same status words, Connect apps, Internet, Relay calls and Peers. On Nov 14, 2026 a desktop node synced with a phone on the same LAN and over the internet — 128 reconciles, 0 errors, stores converged. It ships in the same v0.5.7 release as the phone app.

Calls with no outside servers

OmniCast calls connect directly when they can. Otherwise they relay through another Omni node: desktops relay by default, and phones only if their owner turns on Relay calls, and only while charging on Wi-Fi, with a daily cap. No Google or Cloudflare servers. The relay path between different networks is the part still waiting on its first real-phone trial.

Next up: the Nov 30 cycle

After the Nov 28 STLGameDev playtest (our showcase window is 12–2 PM; builds freeze Nov 22), the next dev cycle fires Nov 30: the split-package migration starting at Omni-Core-UI, envelope encryption for stored events, the OmniMind rebuild, cross-app per-person stats, and a single front door from link → LPE → Halo → Omni Plaza.

Omni Plaza, alive — next slices (roadmap)

The Plaza's living scene — walking animals, flying birds, pond fish, day and night — is in this round's tester build (step 6). Still roadmap: people you meet appearing as living presences, and games as places you walk up to. New anime low-poly Halo figures are the current art direction, with the realistic line on hold.
